The fact that AI is becoming an important part of our lives is evident. We are using it more each day to automate more things, and a lot of the mental work that we used to do will go away; we will have to adapt to this new way of being human, and that is something that will have a significant impact on our lives.

Of course that there are a lot of things that AI can do for us without much effort, and things that can save us from a lot of work, pain, errors, etc, but AI being able to do those things doesn't mean that they are not necessary. We can create software with AI, but the code is still important, we just don't need to look, design, fix errors, and take care of a lot of things that we used to do, but at the end, it is code.

Now imagine that common people start to use AI at the level that developers are using it today, we will have a massive shift on what AI is being used for. People will rely on AI for a lot of common things, decisions, validation, comparison, etc and this will change the way that they think, it will change how the brain of a person works.

> AI will eventually become essential for these people to live, creating a symbiotic relationship.

A kid born into a world where AI takes all the small things, is nothing without AI, the brain adapted to a world where decisions have a different value and meaning, AI will eventually become essential for these people to live, creating a symbiotic relationship.

It will not be essential for "survival in the forest" sense, but without it, living in a modern world would be almost impossible. Things will move fast, new context every hour, memory may slip away with all information at the tip of our tongues, people will interact with each other in a different way… A lot of changes will happen.

This is something really interesting to think about.
